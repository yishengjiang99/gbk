## Short post
GBK Composer is now in ChatGPT. Describe a piece ("a 16-bar Baroque fugue in D minor for organ") and get real,
editable MIDI: revise one passage at a time, see the piano roll, and render WAV + MIDI. No account needed.
Demos: https://compose.grepawk.com/chatgpt/gallery

## Longer post
I built GBK Composer, a music-composition tool you can use from ChatGPT (or any MCP client). It writes structured
MIDI with a deterministic, seeded engine in five styles (baroque, classical, pop, jazz, ambient). It makes targeted edits
(add a walking bass, double the bass an octave lower in bars 9–16, a CC11 crescendo) without touching the rest,
shows an in-chat piano-roll player, and renders WAV in your browser or on its server. Pieces are private by default.
Listen to six engine-made demos: https://compose.grepawk.com/chatgpt/gallery. Source: https://github.com/yishengjiang99/gbk

Accuracy notes for whoever posts: don't claim lyrics or vocals. Don't call the fugue demo a textbook fugue (its
voices enter together). Don't claim ChatGPT availability until the listing is published.
