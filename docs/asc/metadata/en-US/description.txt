GBK Music Composer turns a sentence into a piece of music.

Open the studio, describe what you want to hear, and GBK writes it as real notes you can play, edit and share: a piano piece, a string quartet, a Bach-style fugue. Every note stays editable, and every piece can be exported as WAV or MIDI.

WRITE IT IN CHAT
Tell the composer what to make, then revise it in plain words. Ask for a different key, a slower tempo, a new middle section. The studio keeps up to 10 revisions of a piece on the free plan, so you can always go back to an earlier version.

SCAN SHEET MUSIC
Point the camera at a printed page and GBK reads it into a playable score. Recognition runs on your device. Play the result, fix a note in the piano roll, or keep working on it in the studio.

REAL INSTRUMENT SOUNDS
Pieces play back with SoundFont samples, real recorded instruments rather than synth beeps. Watch the piano roll while a piece plays, read the prompt that produced it, and hear what changed when someone remixed it.

A FEED OF NEW MUSIC
The feed is short pieces other people made. Swipe to listen, leave a star rating or a comment, and open anything in the studio. Every piece shows the prompt behind it. Tap Remix to start from that prompt and publish your version; forks credit the original composer.

WRITE A FUGUE
The built-in fugue generator writes Bach-style counterpoint. Pick a key, a mode and the number of voices, and it composes a subject, answers it, and develops it into a full piece you can edit like any other.

FREE AND PRO
Composing, listening and sharing are free. The free plan keeps 10 revisions per piece, pieces up to 64 bars, and rendered files for 30 days.
GBK Pro raises the limits: 10 times the daily composer budget, 100 revisions per piece, pieces up to 256 bars, rendered files kept for a year, and server renders up to 10 minutes each.
GBK Pro is $5.99 per month or $47.99 per year, each starting with a 7-day free trial. GBK Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase of $99.99.
