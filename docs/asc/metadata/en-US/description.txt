GBK Music Composer is a feed of short musical pieces you can listen to, rate, and remix in a studio.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Swipe through the feed to hear new pieces, each with its piano roll.
2. Open a piece in the studio to see the prompt and notes behind it.
3. Remix it, export WAV or MIDI, and share it back to the feed.

MADE TO BE REMIXED
Every piece shows the prompt that made it. Tap Remix to start from it and make it your own. Forks credit the original composer.

FEATURES
• Vertical audio feed with Following and For You tabs
• Web studio with SoundFont playback and WAV/MIDI export
• Ratings and comments on every piece
• Direct messages and an inbox for replies, forks and comments
• Sign in with Apple or Google
• Block, mute and report for any user or message

WHO IT'S FOR
Composers, songwriters, students and anyone who likes hearing new music and tinkering with it.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Listening and browsing work without an account; posting, rating, comments and messages need one.
• AI and render limits reset daily.
• The first in-browser render downloads a ~32 MB SoundFont.

PRIVACY
We collect only what is needed to run your account and deliver notifications. See https://compose.grepawk.com/privacy. You can delete your account from the support page.
