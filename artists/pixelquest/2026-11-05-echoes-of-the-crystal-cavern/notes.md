# Creative notes
Share: https://compose.grepawk.com/s/echoes-of-the-crystal-cavern-q77rnkn2
- Scene: ice / crystal cavern exploration. Cold shimmer, not a boss fight and not underwater float. 112 BPM, glassy pulse lead, high sparkling 16th arps, soft half-note bass, sparse hats with soft tom rolls.
- Key: A Dorian. The F# over D/Am is the crystal flash (bar 2 of A: F#5–A5–G5–E5; B opens onto a bright D major).
- A hook: A5–C6–B5–E5, then the Dorian F#, descending D6–C6–B5–A5 into G–F#–E. A2 climbs to E6 before B.
- B: brighter climb with C–D–Bm–Em and a high E6 peak; arp and bass push a little harder.
- Whisper: long soft lead tones, sparse 8th arps, soft kick on 1 — an ice-drip breakdown before the A loop.
- v1 → v2: B only lifted 0.3 dB over A, so lead/bass/snare/arp are hotter in B and mix volumes went up (lead 100→108, arp 42→50, bass 92→98). The A-loop seam was a cliff (−32.9 vs −10.4 dB) from a crash on A downbeats and empty end of A; v2 softens those crashes, holds bass through the last beat of A/A-loop, and keeps soft hats into the bar line. Whisper arps denser so the drop isn't hollow.
- v2 render: 95.8s, 44.1k stereo, peak −1.3 dBFS, no clipping. Section RMS: intro −21.0, A −17.5, A2 −17.3, B −16.3, Whisper −23.1, A loop −17.5 dB. Seam end/start −20.5 / −11.6 (much tighter than v1).
