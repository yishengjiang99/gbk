# Creative notes
Share: https://compose.grepawk.com/s/winds-above-the-sky-fortress-cbpb47bz
- Scene: an airship racing up to a fortress in the clouds. 160 BPM, 8th-note octave bass and 16th pulse arps keep it moving; the snare on 2 and 4 with a kick on the 2-and pushes it forward.
- A hook: E-G-B up to A, then the Dorian C# over A in bar 2. A ends on B7 (D# leading tone) to pull back to Em.
- B climbs: C D Bm Em, Am D G, up to the high E and D# over B7. Arp goes up an octave.
- Storm: half-time breakdown with long lead notes, 8th arps, and a borrowed F (bII) before B7. A tom fill brings back the A loop.
- v1 → v2 (after the render check): the intro was as loud as A (-19.5 vs -19.2 dB), so the four-on-the-floor intro kick now ramps up bar by bar. The tom/snare fills were 2 dB hot, so they are about 20 velocity softer. B didn't lift, so a second pulse (Pulse Harmony, panned left) plays 3rds/6ths below the long lead notes in B and the back half of the A loop, B arp and bass are a little louder, and open hats sit on the offbeats. The Storm drop is deeper (arp and bass softer, quieter snare and crash). The bass hits the 5th on the last 8th for more motion.
- v2 render: 67.0s, 44.1k stereo, peak -1.1 dBFS, no clipping, -16.0 LUFS (LRA 3.5). Section RMS: intro -19.4, A -17.1, A2 -17.1, B -16.7, Storm -20.1, A loop -17.1 dB. The loop seam is B7 → Em with a snare roll into a crash on bar 5.
