# Creative notes
Share: https://compose.grepawk.com/s/market-day-in-copperbell-town-c355r7kn
- Scene: bustling town / market square. Cheerful NPC bustle, not a dungeon or battle. 132 BPM with a 2:1 shuffle swing, which no earlier Pixel Quest song used.
- Key: Bb major, also new for the series. The A hook is a repeated-note "market cry" (F5–F5–D5–F5–G5–F5). A ends on F (half cadence), A2 lands on Bb, and B lifts to Eb with a D7 to Gm turn before F7 heads back home.
- Groove: oom-pah bass on 1 and 3 with a swung walk-up on beat 4, pulse stabs on 2 and 4 plus the swung "and" of 4, a shuffle kit with woodblock tick-tock in the intro and cowbell on the and-of-4.
- v1 → v2: v1 was bass-heavy (81% of energy below 250 Hz) with the lead buried, and the loop seam was a cliff (−41.4 vs −10.8 dB). v2 moves the bass up an octave, drops bass volume (100→86) and raises the lead (108→122). It adds a pulse harmony line (3rds/6ths under long notes) in A2/B/A loop and swung 8th bass in B. Pickups and stabs now play legato across the bar line.
- v2 render: 68.7s, peak −1.0 dBFS, no clipping. Section RMS: intro −27.9, A −21.6, A2 −21.8, B −20.9, A loop −21.9 dB. Seam end/start −25.3 / −11.0. Lows went from 81% to 70%. The mp3 got +5 dB through a limiter (mean −17.1, max −0.3 dB).
