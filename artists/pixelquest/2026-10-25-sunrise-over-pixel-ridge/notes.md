# Creative notes
- The mood is a sunny "leaving the village" feeling. The melody climbs through the tonic triad, then answers on V.
- The A section ends on a half cadence, so the loop restarts naturally. B lifts to Bm and borrows bVII (C), which gives a classic chip color. B ends on V with a run up to E6.
- v1 → v2 changes, made after reviewing the render: the lead was buried and every bar had the same RMS (the intro was as loud as the full sections). For v2 I pushed the lead up (vol 114) and pulled the arp down (52, panned right). I softened the intro arp, which now builds into A. I also added up-down arp patterns, echo blips on held lead notes, a bass walk-up every 4th bar, an extra kick on alternate bars, and snare fills at the ends of sections.
- v2 render: 59.3s, 44.1k stereo, no clipping, peak 0.89. GBK loudness reports -17.0 LUFS out (true-peak limited at -1 dBTP).
