# Creative notes
Share: https://compose.grepawk.com/s/lanterns-of-hollowmere-manor-ehyb9zf2
- The scene is a lantern-lit corridor in a crooked manor at midnight. The intro is just the waltz pulse until a lone chromatic line creeps down from D5 to G#4 and resolves to A, as if a door is creaking open.
- A: the hook leans on chromatic neighbour notes (C#–D, E–Eb–D, G#–A, F#–G), and bar 7 slides down six semitones over A7. Every pass ends on an unresolved A major. A2 resolves home to D minor.
- B: it drifts into Bb, then a Picardy-style D major that sounds wrong in a good way, then the Neapolitan-flavoured Eb before it falls back to A. B2 drops the waltz arp and has the second pulse channel echo the lead an octave lower, one eighth late, like a ghost following you.
- Percussion stays sparse and noisy: a soft kick on the downbeat, tick hats, a rimshot on beat 4 in B, and a tom fall at phrase ends.
- v1 → v2 changes, made after reviewing the render: the crash at each section start was a loud transient, so it became a quieter splash. The B sections didn't lift enough, so the lead is louder there. The phrase ends felt static, so the triangle bass now walks down (5th, b5, 4th) into each new section, and the ghost echo comes back in the second half of the A loop.
- v2 render: 67.0s, 44.1k stereo, peak 0.88, no clipping. Bar RMS is about -23 dB for the intro, -20.5 dB for A and -20/-18 dB for B. The loop seam (bar 44 on A back to bar 5 on Dm) is a V–i cadence with matching level (about -19 to -20.8 dB).
