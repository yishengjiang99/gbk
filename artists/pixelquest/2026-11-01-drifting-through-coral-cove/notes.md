# Creative notes
Share: https://compose.grepawk.com/s/drifting-through-coral-cove-zi5xcy8r
- Scene: a diver sprite drifting over a reef. 16th-note arps up and down the chord tones stand in for rising bubbles; reverb and a little chorus add the water.
- A hook: up a major 7th to the high A, then the Lydian B natural over G/F. Bar 6 hangs on B over G before Bbmaj7 → C sets up the loop.
- B: to Bbmaj7 and Gm7, then a borrowed Dbmaj7 (the "deep water" bar) before C7 drops back to F.
- v1 → v2 (after the render check): B didn't lift (same RMS as A), so the lead is louder there, the arp drops every 4th 16th to breathe, and open hats on the offbeats add drive. Closed hats became a softer shaker. The splash at section starts was too hot and is quieter. A quiet "bubble echo" (lead an octave up, three 16ths late) plays on long notes in B and the back half of the A loop. The bass now walks up (2nd, 3rd) into each new section.
- v2 render: 82.6s, 44.1k stereo, peak -1 dBFS, no clipping, about -19 LUFS. Bar RMS: intro -29/-27 dB, A about -21.5, B about -20.5. The loop seam is a V–I (C → Fmaj7) with the bass walk C-D-E into F.
