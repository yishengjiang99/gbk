# Creative notes
Share: https://compose.grepawk.com/s/thunder-lap-at-rustwater-speedway-63z83y23
- Scene: racing stage. Frantic and driving, not a boss fight or a town. 176 BPM in 4/4, the fastest straight-8ths Pixel Quest song after the boss theme.
- Key: F# minor, new for the series. The hook is a fast repeated-note figure (F#5 F#5 A5 G#5 F#5 C#5). A ends on C# (half cadence) and A2 lands on F#m. B lifts to A major for the straightaway, then C#7 heads into the breakdown.
- Intro: a 3-2-1-GO countdown (three A5 beeps, then an A major stack) over a 16th engine-idle pulse, with a bass rev and snare roll into A.
- Groove: octave-bouncing 8th bass, 16th saw arps, kick on 1, the and-of-2 and 3, snare on 2 and 4. Hats go to 16ths in B. C is a breakdown with 16th bass and call/response lead fragments.
- v1 → v2: v1 was bass-heavy (86% of energy below 250 Hz) with the lead buried. C did not dip at all (−19.3 vs −18.4 dB), and the loop seam jumped (−18.3 → −9.8 dB) because of the crash. v2 moves the bass and arps up an octave, lowers bass volume (96→80) and arp volume (70→62), and raises the lead (110→124, +8 velocity in B). It adds a pulse harmony line in A2, B and A loop, softens the kick, and drops to kick-only for the first half of C. The crash is also quieter.
- v2 render: 61.5s, peak −1.0 dBFS, no clipping. Section RMS: intro −24.8, A −19.3, A2 −19.3, B −18.8, C −22.4, A loop −19.2 dB. Seam end/start is now −19.1 / −15.5. Lows dropped from 86% to 73%. The mp3 got +5 dB through a limiter (mean −15.3, max −0.5 dB).
