# The Ferryman's Procession

**Share URL:** https://compose.grepawk.com/s/the-ferryman-s-procession-mfzn9y9a

## Creative notes
First B minor, first 7/8, first cello-led piece and first theme-and-variations in the Maestro Aria catalogue. A ferry crossing a dark river at dusk: a limping 2+2+3 march that remembers warmer days, swells to a full tutti, then fades out.

- **Key / meter / tempo:** B minor, 7/8 (2+2+3), eighth = 210 (♩=105), 60 bars, 2:02 with the held final chord + 4 s tail (2:06).
- **Form:** Intro (1–4) contrabass and timpani tread the 2+2+3 pulse, bassoon pedal, harp → Theme (5–20) cello solo lament over soft string pad, bass tread and harp → Variation I (21–36) the tune remapped into relative D major on clarinet, bassoon 8vb, cellos walking the bass, harp arpeggios; violins 8va and horns join (29–36), timpani roll → Variation II (37–52) B minor tutti march: violins and oboe on the tune, cellos 8vb, trumpets join (45–52), horns and trombones in chorale voicing, timpani tread, Neapolitan C at the peak → Coda (53–60) cello and harp, Neapolitan and V7, Picardy close on B major.
- **Lead colour:** Cello; clarinet carries Variation I, violins and oboe the tutti.
- **Craft:** Four-part pad found by voice-leading search. Hard bans on same-direction parallel 5ths/8ves between melody, pad voices and bass (eighth-note grid): 0 found. Leading tones resolve up, sevenths down; no doubled leading tone even with the melody. No pad crossings. Ranges: cello D2–G5, clarinet D4–B5, bassoon E2–B4, violins B4–B6, trumpets B3–G5, contrabass E1–D2, timpani E2–G3. Render MIDI matches project note-for-note (1225 notes).
