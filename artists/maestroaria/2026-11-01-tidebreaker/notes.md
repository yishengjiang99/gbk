# Creative notes: Tidebreaker (Maestro Aria)

**Share URL:** https://compose.grepawk.com/s/tidebreaker-qwpxq8xv

- **Character:** a driving, stormy cue in G minor and asymmetric 5/4 (3+2), ♩=132, 52 bars, ~2:01 with tail. Brass-led, unlike the earlier woodwind/string-led pieces (D-minor quartet adagio, E-flat 4/4 heroic cue, C#-minor 6/8 nocturne, E-minor 3/4 scherzo).
- **Intro (1–6):** cello/contrabass ostinato in 3+3+2+2 eighths, timpani, a hushed choir pad, roll into the theme.
- **A (7–22):** horns state the theme over pulsing violin II/viola and trombone chords. Violin I joins at the octave in the second half. A Neapolitan A-flat chord sets up the half cadence; F7 pivots to B-flat.
- **B (23–34):** lyrical B-flat-major glow. Long violin line, two-part choir, horn counterline, sustained low strings. A timpani roll and trumpet call lead back.
- **Climax (35–46):** trumpet theme fortissimo with horns 8vb, violin descant, full brass and choir, timpani on every accent, a second Neapolitan before V7.
- **Coda (47–52):** horn echo over a thinning ostinato, a tutti G-minor hit with a timpani roll, then a final low unison G.
