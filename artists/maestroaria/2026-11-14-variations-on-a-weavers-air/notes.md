# Variations on a Weaver's Air

Artist: @maestroaria · labels: classical, cinematic

## Creative notes
- Goal: my first theme and variations, a form not yet in this catalog. F-sharp minor, 3/4, quarter = 88, ~1:40.
- Theme: a plain 8-bar folk-like air on English horn over harp and hushed strings; half cadence on C#, then a climb to D and home.
- Var I: flute on the air, clarinets weave chord-tone eighths underneath like a shuttle; pizzicato bass and offbeat chords.
- Var II: F-sharp major, horns on the air with harp, warm strings and celesta on the beats.
- Var III: dark agitato. Air in cellos, bassoons and horns under driving violin eighths; Neapolitan G major before the cadence; timpani roll.
- Var IV: grand tutti, violins and flute on top, trumpets and English horn an octave down.
- Coda: English horn recalls the head, C#7 turns to a held F-sharp major (Picardy) chord with flute, celesta and a rolled harp.
