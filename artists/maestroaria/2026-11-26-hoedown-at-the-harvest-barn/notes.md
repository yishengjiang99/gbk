# Hoedown at the Harvest Barn — notes

Americana hoedown in cut time 2/2, half note = 84, D major rising to E major, 76 bars, ~1:50. A Thanksgiving piece. First hoedown, first Americana color, first 2/2 since the Cartographer overture, and the first solo fiddle, xylophone and wood blocks in the series.

Orchestra: flute, clarinet, horns, trumpets, trombones, timpani, xylophone, percussion (wood blocks, snare, bass drum, cymbal), solo fiddle (violin), pizzicato strings, strings, contrabass. No harp, choir or tuba.

Form: stomp intro, fiddle tune A and A', brass tune B, tutti A, a quiet G major hymn of thanks, a rising build to B7, the whole dance again in E major, coda with borrowed A minor and a stinger.

## Drafts and critique

1. **Hoedown at the Harvest Barn, D major, 2/2, half 84.** Hook: strongest, a syncopated D–F#–A–D fiddle leap. Harmony: clean, 0 checker issues; G-to-E lift into the key change, borrowed iv in the coda. Form: richest arc (dance, hymn, build, key change), 1:49. Groove: pizzicato boom-chick plus wood blocks, the best drive. Style: fresh cinematic Americana. Weak spots: A came back unchanged four times, thin intro, 20 notes at velocity 125+, 7 pizzicato notes out of range.
2. **Pavane for the Gleaners, E-flat major, 4/2, half 56.** Hook: gentle falling G–F–E-flat, modest. Harmony: nice modal D-flat/B-flat minor and A-flat minor color, but 7 accented non-chord tones. Form: 4-bar phrases only, 1:43, abrupt end. Groove: static tabor rhythm, busy harp. Style: too close to the sarabande and processional; weak low end (sub band -41.6 dB).
3. **Galop of the Runaway Hay Wagons, F major, 2/4, quarter 138.** Hook: bouncy but generic arpeggio. Harmony: 0 issues, trio in D-flat. Form: clear but only 1:13, A heard five times. Groove: frantic oom-pah, fun. Style: more circus than cinematic, and too close to yesterday's march (snare, oom-pah).

**Winner: draft 1.** Best hook and groove, the fullest form, and new ground for the catalog.
