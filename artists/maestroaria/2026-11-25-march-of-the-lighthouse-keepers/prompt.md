# Prompt: March of the Lighthouse Keepers

Original cinematic march for orchestra in C major, 4/4, quarter = 116, 56 bars (~2:00). Mood: lighthouse keepers marching out along a sea wall at dawn; brisk and proud, with a warm, homesick trio that comes back in full glory. Form: march with trio. Fanfare (1–4) trumpets, horns and trombones in octaves, snare and bass drum, a borrowed A-flat–B-flat turn, snare roll on G7. March A (5–12) trumpets state the hook, a dotted C–E–G rise that falls back, over tuba and contrabass oom-pah, off-beat horns and strings, light snare; horns echo the head. March A' (13–20) violins and flute take the tune an octave up, horns sing a countermelody, cadence climbs to a high C. Trio (21–36) in F major: horns and cellos sing a legato tune, clarinet counterline, soft strings; the second half turns to D minor, a borrowed A-flat–B-flat lift and D7–G7, building with a snare roll and timpani. March tutti (37–44) the hook in trumpets, violins, flute and glockenspiel, cello and trombone counterline. Grandioso (45–52) the trio tune returns in C major for full orchestra. Coda (53–56) F to F minor (borrowed iv), G7, a three-hit C major stinger and a final tutti chord with cymbal and timpani roll. Flute, clarinet, horns, trumpets, trombones, tuba, timpani, glockenspiel, percussion (snare, bass drum, cymbal), strings, cellos, contrabass. Bass and inner voices searched under strict voice-leading rules (no parallel fifths or octaves, sevenths resolve down, idiomatic ranges). No solo piano or string quartet; no quoted melodies.
