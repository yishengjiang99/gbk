# March of the Lighthouse Keepers — notes

**Share URL:** https://compose.grepawk.com/s/march-of-the-lighthouse-keepers-zg3x4gnc

C major march in 4/4, quarter = 116, 56 bars, ~2:00. First march with trio, first C major tonic and first snare-drum part in the series. Bright and brisk after yesterday's slow G-sharp minor lament.

Orchestra: flute, clarinet, horns, trumpets, trombones, tuba, timpani, glockenspiel, percussion (snare, bass drum, cymbal), strings, cellos, contrabass. No harp, choir or English horn this time; brass and snare carry the color.

Form: fanfare, march A (trumpets), A' (violins, horn counter), trio in F (horns and cellos), trio build through D minor and A-flat–B-flat, march tutti, grandioso trio in C, coda with borrowed F minor and a stinger.

## Drafts and critique

1. **March of the Lighthouse Keepers, C major, 4/4, quarter 116.** Hook: strongest of the three, a dotted C–E–G rise that falls back. Harmony: clean diatonic with borrowed A-flat–B-flat, secondary A7 and D7; 0 checker issues. Form: clear march and trio, but A came back unchanged and the trio never returned. Groove: firm oom-pah and snare, the best drive. Style: heroic film march. Audio: only 1:39, LRA 4.8 (flat).
2. **Berceuse for the Snow Owl, E minor, 3/4, quarter 66.** Hook: gentle rocking tune, not memorable. Harmony: plain i–iv–V7, E major close; 8 melody non-chord tones on beats. Form: A B A coda, only 1:31. Groove: static harp eighths. Style: soft and pretty but close to the nocturne and aubade; intro too quiet (-37 dB RMS).
3. **Tango of the Midnight Clockworks, D minor, 4/4, quarter 112.** Hook: decent leap A–D–F. Harmony: Neapolitan E-flat, 0 issues. Form: A heard three times, only 1:24. Groove: 3+3+2 bass never varies. Style: too close to the habanera two days ago; flat dynamics (LRA 4.0).

**Winner: draft 1.** Best hook and drive, cleanest harmony, and the newest form, key and color in the catalog.
