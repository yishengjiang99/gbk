## Creative notes
- Goal: a chaconne in 5/4 (3+2), a form and meter not yet in this catalog. G minor, quarter = 76, ~2:15.
- Color: brass chorale with harp. Horns, trumpets, trombones and tuba carry the tune; harp and tubular bells add frost.
- Ground: four bars, Gm–Eb | Cm–D | Bb–Eb | Cm–D, chords on beats 1 and 4. It repeats under every variation.
- Ground alone: cellos and basses, quarter-note harp, one bell on G, soft horns.
- Chorale: horn hymn over trombones, tuba and harp.
- Relative major: ground turns to B-flat; trumpet song over running harp eighths and pizzicato bass.
- Agitato: tune in cellos and trombones, string eighths, horn stabs on 1 and 4, timpani roll on D.
- G major: flute and bells over soft horns and harp, closing on D7.
- Tutti and climax: full brass chorale, trumpets on top, Neapolitan A-flat before D7.
- Coda: horns recall the tune, plagal Cm–Gm close, held G minor chord with bell, rolled harp and fading timpani.
