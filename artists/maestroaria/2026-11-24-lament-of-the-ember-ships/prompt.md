# Prompt: Lament of the Ember Ships

Mood: burning ships drifting out on a night tide; grief that rises to a full-orchestra cry, then a quiet major-key farewell. Form: Intro (1–4) G-sharp pedal in basses and cellos, rippling harp, hushed choir, flute glints, timpani roll into D-sharp 7. A (5–12) English horn hook, a long sighing descent D-sharp–C-sharp–B–A-sharp–B–G-sharp, answered by a climb to B and a half cadence; second phrase turns through the Neapolitan A major and F-double-sharp. B (13–20) B major episode: cellos sing, clarinet countermelody, horns, harp in quarter notes against the dotted-half pulse, climbing to C-sharp over A major, choir and trombones swelling into the return. A' (21–28) tutti: violins, flute, horns and choir on the hook, cello and bassoon counterline, trombones, timpani. Coda (29–33) hook fragments through A major and D-sharp 7, then a Picardy G-sharp major chord with a harp sweep. Flute, English horn, clarinet, bassoon, horns, trombones, timpani, harp, choir, strings, cellos, contrabass.
