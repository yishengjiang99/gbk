# Lament of the Ember Ships — notes

**Share URL:** https://compose.grepawk.com/s/lament-of-the-ember-ships-wqukaven

G-sharp minor lament in 9/4, quarter = 120, 33 bars, ~2:30. First lament, first 9/4 and first G-sharp minor tonic in the series. Slow and grieving after yesterday's E-flat minor habanera in 2/4.

Orchestra: flute, English horn, clarinet, bassoon, horns, trombones, timpani, harp, choir, strings, cellos, contrabass. English horn and wordless choir are the signature colors.

Form: intro on a G-sharp pedal, A (English horn), B in B major (cellos, clarinet counter), tutti A', coda through the Neapolitan to a Picardy G-sharp major chord.

## Drafts and critique

1. **Lament of the Ember Ships, G-sharp minor, 9/4, quarter 120.** Hook: long sighing descent with a climbing answer; the most singable of the three. Harmony: Neapolitan A major, F-double-sharp leading tone, Picardy end; 0 checker issues. Form: clear intro A B A' coda arc, LRA 7.0. Groove: harp eighths never vary; slow but breathing. Style: strongest film-elegy fit. Audio: intro too dark and quiet (-35 dB RMS), top band dull (2–8 kHz -15.2 dB).
2. **Gigue of the Clockmaker's Daughter, F-sharp major, 3/8, dotted quarter 68.** Hook: bouncy but short-breathed. Harmony: plain I–IV–V, 2 parallel octaves/fifths in the B major episode. Form: A heard three times nearly unchanged; only 1:28. Groove: lively lilt, the best of the three. Style: more classical than cinematic, close to the earlier scherzo and overture.
3. **Pavane for the Lantern Bridge, C major, 4/2, half 63.** Hook: noble dactyl opening, then stodgy straight halves. Harmony: nice flat-side turn (B-flat, A-flat) and bVI–bVII coda; 0 issues. Form: B too short. Groove: stately but static. Style: brass chorale close to the processional and vespers; flat dynamics (LRA 4.1).

**Winner: draft 1.** Best hook and emotional arc, cleanest voice leading, and the newest form, meter and key in the catalog.

## Revision

Louder intro with soft high strings and flute glints on the top voice. High string octave and flute echoes in A, upper string octave in the tutti, to brighten the top (2–8 kHz -15.2 to -14.6 dB). Harp in B switched to quarter notes against the dotted-half pulse, so the episode moves differently from A.
