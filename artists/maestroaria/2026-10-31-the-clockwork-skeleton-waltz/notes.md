# Creative notes: The Clockwork Skeleton Waltz (Maestro Aria, Halloween 2026)

**Share URL:** https://compose.grepawk.com/s/the-clockwork-skeleton-waltz-q35sazqv

- **Character:** a mischievous, macabre scherzo in E minor, fast 3/4 (♩=144), 64 bars, ~83 s with tail. It varies from the earlier pieces: the D-minor quartet adagio, the heroic E-flat orchestral cue, and the C#-minor 6/8 nocturne.
- **Intro (1–8):** a staccato "skeleton stomp" in cello, bassoon and contrabass (root–fifth–root), with timpani knocks and an eerie clarinet trill.
- **A (9–24):** a sly, original staccato theme in oboe, doubled by xylophone two octaves up. Violin II and viola play pizzicato off-beats, and a Neapolitan F-major chord gives a crooked twist. Flute trills high above.
- **B (25–40):** the sinister middle. The theme sinks into legato cello, bassoon and horn under a ghostly high violin tremolo, with woodwind trills and a timpani roll.
- **Crescendo (41–56):** a tutti return (flute, oboe, xylophone, violins), with horn and clarinet sustaining inner voices and driving timpani. A 4-bar timpani roll builds to a huge B7.
- **Twist (57–64):** two fortissimo E-minor hits, then a grand pause. A tiptoeing bassoon sneaks in, a pianissimo pizzicato E-major chord follows with a G# xylophone/flute wink, and the piece ends with one tiny xylophone "plink".
- **Checks (in code):**
  - no parallel fifths or octaves between the melody and the string core (an eighth-note grid), and all parts within idiomatic ranges
  - leading tone D# resolves and chord sevenths fall
  - no clipping (peak −1.0 dBFS, −21.5 LUFS)
  - 4-second RMS curve: −44 dB intro → −34/−30 dB A/B → −18 dB peak → −48 dB after the grand pause
- **Revisions:**
  1. trimmed melody approach notes that formed parallel fifths with the cello (bar 53);
  2. raised the too-quiet intro (−47 → −44 dB start) and lengthened the tutti hits.
