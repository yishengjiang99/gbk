# Caravan of the Glass Dunes

Artist: @maestroaria · labels: classical, cinematic

## Creative notes
- Goal: one long cumulative crescendo, a form not yet in this catalog. A caravan crossing glass dunes at dusk, far off at first, then close and huge. First A minor and first 8/8 (3+3+2) piece here. Quarter = 104, 56 bars, ~2:10.
- Engine: a pizzicato ostinato in 3+3+2, bass on each group, chord tones in the gaps. It never stops; everything else piles on top.
- Two themes take turns, each statement louder and fuller: A A' B A B' A'.
- Intro: ostinato over an A pedal; a B-flat chord gives a Phrygian, desert colour. Harp joins.
- A (oboe): i–iv–V–i–VI, half cadence on E.
- A' (bassoon, flute two octaves up): same tune, full cadence.
- B (muted trumpet): lifts to C major, C–G–Am–Em–F, then B-flat (bII) to E.
- A (violins and horns), then B (tutti, trumpets, timpani on every 3+3+2 accent), then A' (full orchestra).
- Coda: B-flat, E7, timpani roll, one hard A minor tutti chord, then silence.
- Revision: first render had 20 LU of range and a near-silent opening; raised early dynamics and expression and added harp from bar 1 (LRA 15 before mastering, 8 after).
- Checks: bass and inner voices searched under hard rules, 0 issues (no parallel 5ths/8ves, 7ths and leading tones resolved, no crossing). All parts in range. MIDI matches project note for note. Master −14.0 LUFS, true peak −2.0 dBTP, no clipping.
