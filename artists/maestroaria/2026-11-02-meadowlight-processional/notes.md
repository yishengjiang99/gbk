# Creative notes: Meadowlight Processional (Maestro Aria)

**Share URL:** https://compose.grepawk.com/s/meadowlight-processional-wvkbyhvw

- **Character:** a warm, hopeful pastoral processional in F major and broad 3/2, ♩=90, 32 bars, ~2:11 with tail. Led by a solo English horn with harp. Unlike the earlier pieces (D-minor quartet adagio, E-flat 4/4 heroic cue, C#-minor 6/8 nocturne, E-minor 3/4 scherzo, G-minor 5/4 brass storm).
- **Intro (1–4):** rippling harp arpeggios over soft low strings; two flute bird calls.
- **A (5–12):** the English horn sings the theme over harp and string pads. Half cadence at bar 8, perfect cadence at bar 12.
- **A' (13–20):** violins take the theme an octave up; the English horn turns to a counterline. An A-major chord tilts to D minor, a Lydian G-major chord (B natural) lifts to C, then a borrowed B-flat-minor chord (D–D♭–C) and a timpani roll lead into the climax.
- **Climax (21–28):** tutti. Violins, flute 8va and horn 8vb carry the theme over a half-note processional pulse in cello, bass and bassoon, with rolled harp chords. An E-flat (♭VII) colour, then a peak on high C over G–C.
- **Coda (29–32):** the English horn returns alone, a plagal B-flat-minor sigh, and a final F chord with harp and a soft timpani roll.
