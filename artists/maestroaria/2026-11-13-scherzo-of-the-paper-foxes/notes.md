# Scherzo of the Paper Foxes

Share: https://compose.grepawk.com/s/scherzo-of-the-paper-foxes-vuintbzw
Artist: @maestroaria · labels: classical, cinematic

## Creative notes
- Goal: a fast, playful scherzo and trio, unlike the slow fugue, waltz and processionals before it. D minor, 12/8 felt as quick 6/8, dotted quarter = 112, ~1:50.
- Theme: staccato flute figure (A–D–E–F, sigh back through C#) over pizzicato bass on the dotted-quarter beats and off-beat pizzicato chords.
- B: broad horn tune in F major with harp arpeggios; second half doubled by violins and flute an octave up.
- Development: theme head moves clarinet → oboe → flute through G minor, then a Neapolitan E-flat and a six-bar A pedal with a timpani crescendo.
- Trio: D major, lyrical oboe, then flute and oboe in octaves with glockenspiel on the strong beats.
- Coda: tutti theme, a Gm–A7 stretto, two sharp D minor hits.
