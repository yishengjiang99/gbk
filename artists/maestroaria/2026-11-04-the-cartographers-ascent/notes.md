# Creative notes: The Cartographer's Ascent (Maestro Aria)

**Share URL:** https://compose.grepawk.com/s/the-cartographer-s-ascent-q75a9zay

- **Idea:** a mapmaker climbing toward the edge of the known world. First adventure overture in this catalogue, first in A major and first in alla breve (2/2).
- **Colour:** borrowed bVI and bVII (F and G) give the horn call and the ending their film-score lift; the middle section drops straight into F major for a lyrical solo violin and oboe tune, then turns back to A through G and E7.
- **Arc:** galloping violin ostinato and timpani → horn call → trumpet fanfare theme with horns → second phrase adds flute, trombone chorale and strings → hushed F-major "far country" with glockenspiel → long crescendo and timpani roll → full tutti summit (trumpets and oboe, horns an octave below, flute and glockenspiel above) → D–F–G–A coda and a final roll.
- **Checks:** four-part pad voicings found by search under hard rules; no parallel fifths/octaves on an eighth-note grid between melody, pad voices and bass; leading tones and sevenths resolve; melody stays above the pad; all parts in range.
- **Revisions:** moved the theme's first note up to E5 so the pad could sit under it; rewrote seven melody spots that clashed with the rules (a Bm7–E7 seventh doubling the melody's G#, two leading tones moving in octaves with the melody, Bb–C and F–G octaves with the bass, two hidden fifths at phrase joins); dropped the intro horn call's high F#; removed two timpani hits that doubled the start of rolls. Re-rendered and confirmed the MIDI matches the project note for note.
- **Final MP3:** −15.0 LUFS integrated, peak −1.0 dBFS, no clipped samples, LRA 15.7 LU, ~1:41.
