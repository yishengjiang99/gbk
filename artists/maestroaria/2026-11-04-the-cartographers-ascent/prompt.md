# Prompt: The Cartographer's Ascent

Original adventure overture for orchestra in A major, alla breve 2/2, half note = 80, 63 bars (~1:40). Mood: bright, wind-swept, heroic, a mapmaker climbing toward the edge of the known world. Form: Intro (1–8) galloping violin ostinato over an A pedal, timpani, then a horn call through D, F and G (borrowed bVI–bVII) to a timpani roll on E7 → Theme (9–24) fanfare trumpet theme (E–A–C#–E) over horns and the gallop, a half cadence, then a second phrase with flute an octave up, trombone chorale and strings → Far Country (25–40) sudden shift to F major: a lyrical solo violin and oboe melody with glockenspiel sparkle, then Bb–C–Am–Dm–Bb–G turning back through E7 with a building timpani roll → Summit (41–56) full tutti: theme in trumpets, oboe, horns an octave below, flute and glockenspiel an octave above, tuba and trombones, ending D–F–G–A → Coda (57–63) bVI–bVII–I fanfare and a final timpani roll. Flute, oboe, trumpets, horns, trombones, tuba, timpani, glockenspiel, strings, violins, cellos, contrabass. Four-part voice leading searched under hard rules (no parallel fifths/octaves between melody, inner voices and bass; leading tones and sevenths resolved; idiomatic ranges). No solo piano or string quartet; no quoted melodies.
