Original bright, gusty rondo for orchestra in G major, 5/8 (3+2), eighth = 160, 56 bars (~1:45 plus tail). Mood: kites tugging on the wind over white salt flats; a skipping refrain that keeps coming back, two contrasting episodes, and a sharp tutti ending. Form: rondo A–B–A–C–A with coda. Intro (1–4) pizzicato strings in a 3+2 lilt on a G pedal, flute gust runs, soft timpani, a roll on D7 → Refrain (5–12) oboe theme over pizzicato, flute an octave up in the second phrase → Episode I (13–20) E minor: legato clarinet with bassoon an octave below, flowing cello eighths, soft horns, oboe echoes → Refrain II (21–28) violins with flute 8va and oboe, clarinet arpeggios, pizzicato and bassoon on the beats → Episode II (29–40) a broad turn to E-flat major (bVI): violins with horns 8vb, trumpets join, high flute, Neapolitan A-flat and C minor (iv) lead through D7 with a growing timpani roll → Refrain III (41–48) full tutti → Coda (49–56) oboe over pizzicato with a C-minor sigh and an E-flat colour, crescendo through D7 to a timpani-rolled G and one short tutti hit with a last pizzicato pluck.
