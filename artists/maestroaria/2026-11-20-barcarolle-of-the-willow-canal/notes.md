## Drafts and critique
1. **Barcarolle in G-flat major** (this piece). Hook: a gentle arch tune, singable. Harmony: richest of the three (relative minor, chromatic-mediant D major, borrowed C-flat minor). Form: clear A A' B B2 A'' coda arc. Groove: steady rocking 6/8. Style: romantic film score. Weakness: quiet opening, horn underused in A', celesta thin in the tutti.
2. **March of the Bridge Builders, C major** (4/4, 112, trio in A-flat). Strong drive and a bVI–bVII hook, but oom-pah accompaniment sounds like a band, dynamics flat (LRA 4), and too close to yesterday's discarded march.
3. **Elegy for the Last Lamplighter, E-flat minor** (4/4, 60, 30 bars). Expressive, with a C-flat major climax. But it is slow D-minor-adagio territory again (Lantern Adagio), sparse, and a timpani roll fell out of range.

**Winner: draft 1.** Freshest form and key for the catalog, and the best harmonic arc.
