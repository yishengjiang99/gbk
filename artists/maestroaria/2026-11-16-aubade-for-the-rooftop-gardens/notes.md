## Creative notes
- Goal: a sunrise aubade built on an additive ostinato, unlike the grounds, fugues and dances before it. First D-flat major and first 7/4 (4+3) piece here. Quarter = 84, ~2:33.
- Color: celesta and marimba ostinato with harp; clarinet and flute carry the song. Brass waits for the sunrise.
- Dawn: celesta eighths in 4+3 over harp and hushed strings.
- Clarinet song: Db–Gb | Bbm–Gb | Db–Ebm | Gb–Ab.
- Flute song: marimba joins, clarinet holds a line beneath, ends on Ab7.
- Shadow: B-flat minor. Horns and cellos on the tune over a string ostinato; F major as V; a wistful G-flat minor chord.
- Build: violins and flute climb over borrowed A major (bVI) chords; timpani roll.
- Sunrise: full tutti, trumpets, horns, strings and flute; peak on G-flat 6.
- Coda: clarinet returns; minor iv (G-flat minor) to Db; held chord, rolled harp, high celesta A-flat.
- Checks: bass and inner voices searched under hard rules, 0 issues (no parallel 5ths/8ves, 7ths and leading tones resolved, no crossing). All parts in range. MIDI matches project note for note. Master −15.2 LUFS, peak −2.0 dBFS, no clipping.
