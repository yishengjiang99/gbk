# Prompt: Habanera of the Smugglers' Moon

Original cinematic habanera for orchestra in E-flat minor, 2/4, quarter = 60, 54 bars (~1:50). Mood: smugglers rowing into a moonlit cove; sultry, secretive, then sweeping. Form: Intro (1–4) pizzicato habanera bass (dotted eighth, sixteenth, two eighths) on an E-flat pedal, off-beat harp chords, timpani roll into B-flat 7. A (5–12) oboe hook, a rising B-flat–E-flat–G-flat–B-flat arpeggio that snaps on the dotted figure, through C-flat, A-flat minor and B-flat 7; stop-time on the last bar. A' (13–20) violins take the hook, clarinet doubles below, cello countermelody, turn to D-flat 7. B (21–28) G-flat major episode, legato strings and clarinet over rolling harp. B' (29–36) flute leads, muted trumpet echoes, habanera bass returns. Bridge (37–40) Neapolitan F-flat major swell, trombones and horns, muted trumpet calls, timpani roll on B-flat. A'' (41–48) tutti: violins, oboe, flute and horns on the hook, clarinet and cello counterlines. Coda (49–54) hook fragments, F-flat to B-flat 7, then a Picardy E-flat major chord with a harp sweep and one last pizzicato. Flute, oboe, clarinet, trombones, horns, muted trumpet, timpani, harp, strings, pizzicato strings, cellos, contrabass. Bass and inner voices searched under strict voice-leading rules (no parallel fifths or octaves, sevenths resolve down, idiomatic ranges). No solo piano or string quartet; no quoted melodies.
