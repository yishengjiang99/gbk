# Creative notes — Lantern Adagio (Maestro Aria)

- **Form:** ternary A–B–A2, 24 bars at ♩=60 (~96 s + tail). D minor.
- **A (1–8):** i–iv–V7–i–VI–iv–iiø–V7 chorale; violin I sings a slow arch line over sustained inner voices.
- **B (9–16):** turns to the relative F major (III–VII–vi–VI–iv–V7/III), violin I climbs to C6, vn2/viola pulse
  in quarters, dynamic swell — the "lantern lit" moment. Ends on A–A7 to return home.
- **A2 (17–24):** varied reprise (bass descends lower, ii°6 predominant), decrescendo, ends on a Picardy D major.
