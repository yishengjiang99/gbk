# Toccata of the Thunder Looms — notes

**Share URL:** https://compose.grepawk.com/s/toccata-of-the-thunder-looms-9iyjekd4

B-flat major toccata in 11/8 (3+3+3+2), eighth = 300, 54 bars, ~2:00. First toccata, first 11/8 and first B-flat major tonic in the series. Fast and bright, after two slow dances (Barcarolle, Sarabande).

Orchestra: flute, clarinet, bassoon, horns, trumpets, trombones, tuba, timpani, glockenspiel, strings, cellos, contrabass. No oboe or harp this time; tuba and glockenspiel are new colors.

Form: intro on a B-flat pedal, A (trumpets), A' (violins, horn counter), B (G minor to E-flat, flute and glockenspiel), bridge on an F pedal, theme lifted to D-flat major, tutti A'', coda with a bVI–bVII–V–I climb.

## Drafts and critique

1. **Toccata, B-flat major, 11/8.** Hook: strong, a leaping F–B-flat–D figure that snaps on the short group. Harmony: diatonic with a D-flat lift and borrowed bVI–bVII coda; zero parallels, passing tones only on eighths. Form: clear, but the theme comes four times. Groove: relentless drive, B section gives the only rest. Style: action-score brass and ostinato. Audio: -15 LUFS, LRA 4.8 (narrow), low-mids slightly thick.
2. **Pavane, D-flat major, 2/2.** Hook: gentle, not memorable. Harmony: smooth, nice borrowed iv at the end. Form: tidy arch. Groove: stately but too close in mood to yesterday's Sarabande. Style: English horn and celesta are lovely. Audio: thin sub (-32 dB band), LRA 6.0.
3. **Habanera, G-sharp minor, 2/4.** Hook: decent dotted figure. Harmony: Neapolitan A major works; the B-major middle is plain sequence. Form: A B A coda, very square. Groove: sultry but the habanera bass never varies. Style: fits film noir more than orchestral cinematic. Audio: dark (2–8 kHz band -15.6 dB), muddy low-mids.

**Winner: draft 1.** Freshest form, meter and key in the catalog, with the strongest hook.

## Revision

Softer intro and B section for more contrast (LRA 4.8 to 5.5). Horns foreshadow the hook in bar 4. A' now ends on D7 to turn into G minor. Lighter cello pulse and trombone stabs to thin the low-mids. Glockenspiel accents in the final tutti.

## Final

-15.0 LUFS, true peak -1.9 dBTP, no clipped samples, all parts in range, no parallel fifths or octaves.
