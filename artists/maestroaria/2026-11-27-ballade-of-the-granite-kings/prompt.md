# Prompt: Ballade of the Granite Kings

Original cinematic ballade for orchestra in D Dorian (D minor), 4/2, quarter = 120 (half = 60), 39 bars (~2:40). Mood: the old granite kings asleep under the mountain; a story told by firelight, dark and noble, swelling to a great low-brass climax, ending in a warm D major. Low-register writing throughout: melodies in cello, horn, viola, clarinet and trombone, nothing high or shrill; contrabasses and tuba double the bass an octave down. Form: Horn call (1–4) over a low D pedal and timpani roll, foreshadowing the hook. Cello ballad (5–12) solo cellos sing the theme (rising D–A fifth, falling back through the Dorian B natural) over low harp and soft strings. Horns (13–20) horns and violas take the theme, cellos and bassoons add a counterline. F major episode (21–28) clarinet and violas in a warmer, rising strain through B-flat and E-flat to A, choir hum, walking contrabass, timpani roll into the climax. Climax (29–36) theme in horns, cellos, violas and trombones an octave down, full choir and strings, tuba and timpani. Coda (37–39) G minor to A7, then D major with a rolled harp chord. Clarinet, bassoons, horns, trombones, tuba, timpani, harp, choir, violas, strings, cellos, contrabasses. Bass and inner voices searched under strict voice-leading rules (no parallel fifths or octaves, sevenths resolve down, idiomatic ranges). No solo piano or string quartet; no quoted melodies.
