# Creative notes: Signal Fire on the Headland (Maestro Aria)

**Share URL:** https://compose.grepawk.com/s/signal-fire-on-the-headland-2y49pd5f

- **Idea:** a coastal beacon at night — wind on the headland, one fire answering ships. First E major piece in this catalogue, and the first straight 4/4 allegretto lighthouse/beacon cue (not a heroic overture, not pastoral).
- **Colour:** borrowed bVI (C major) colours the intro horn call and the coda; the middle sea swell sits in relative C# minor and brushes a Neapolitan F before the timpani roll.
- **Arc:** muted violin pulse and soft timpani → distant horn call → horns state a rising B–E–G#–B beacon motif over string ostinato, clarinet then flute/trumpet answering → darker clarinet and low-string swell → tutti climax (trumpets, horns, flute 8va, harp, trombones, timpani) → horn echo and a final soft E-major harp glow.
- **Checks:** four-part pad voicings found by search under hard rules; no parallel fifths/octaves on an eighth-note grid between melody, pad voices and bass; leading tones and sevenths resolve; melody stays above the pad; all parts in range. 1233 notes across flute, oboe, clarinet, horns, trumpets, trombones, timpani, harp, strings, violins, cellos, contrabass.
- **Revisions:** raised several theme and swell melody starts so the pad had room; nudged twelve bar-join melody pitches that made root–bass or fifth parallels (including the C→G#m join into the late swell); octave-raised nine mid-phrase notes that dipped under the pad S; remastered with +8.5 dB makeup after a first pass landed too quiet (−22 LUFS).
- **Final MP3:** −16.2 LUFS integrated, true peak −3.0 dBFS, no clipped samples, LRA 11.1 LU, ~2:21.
