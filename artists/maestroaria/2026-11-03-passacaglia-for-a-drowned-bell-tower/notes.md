# Creative notes: Passacaglia for a Drowned Bell Tower (Maestro Aria)

**Share URL:** https://compose.grepawk.com/s/passacaglia-for-a-drowned-bell-tower-6ghnbxpy

- **Idea:** a bell tower under the sea. A two-bar lament ground (Bb–A–Ab–G–Gb–F) repeats 14 times; the music rises around it and sinks again. First passacaglia/ground form in this catalogue, first in B-flat minor and 12/8.
- **Harmonizations of the ground:** minor (Bbm, F/A, Fm/Ab, Eb/G, Ebm/Gb, F), a darker one (A°7, Ab, Eb7 with Dorian G, Gb), and a major one for the climax (Bb, Bb7/Ab, F7).
- **Arc:** bass and a distant bell → solo cello theme → clarinet and cello trade lines → flute/clarinet octaves over 12/8 string arpeggios → horn, then violins with trombone chorale → major-mode climax with bells and timpani, peak on high G-flat → sudden pianissimo in minor → Picardy B-flat-major ending.
- **Checks:** no parallel fifths/octaves on an eighth-note grid between melody, pad voices, bass and counterlines; leading tones and sevenths resolve; all parts in range.
- **Revisions:** fixed five melodic passing tones that made parallel fifths with the bass or tenor (Db over Gb–F, G over Ab–G, D–C over Ab–G); moved the clarinet arpeggio up an octave where it fell below its range; after the first render (−17.2 LUFS, LRA 24.6 LU, intro at −46 dB) raised the quiet sections and flattened the expression curve, re-rendered, then mastered with light compression and a limiter.
- **Final MP3:** −15.0 LUFS integrated, true peak −1.9 dBTP, LRA 10.0 LU, ~2:18.
