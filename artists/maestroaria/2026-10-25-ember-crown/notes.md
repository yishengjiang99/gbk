# Creative notes — Ember Crown (Maestro Aria)

**Share URL:** https://compose.grepawk.com/s/ember-crown-rmpaqyj8

- **Key / tempo:** E-flat major, ♩=80, 4/4, 41 bars (~2:07 with tail). A heroic counterpart to the D-minor *Lantern Adagio*.
- **Intro (1–8):** C-minor colour (Cm–Ab–Eb–Bb). A solo horn sings the theme over sustained divisi strings, with soft timpani rolls on C and a dominant roll into the build.
- **Build (9–20):** the violins take the theme, doubled by oboe and later flute an octave up. Viola and violin II pulse in eighths, cello in quarters. A borrowed D-flat (bVII) chord adds a filmic lift, and a timpani crescendo roll sets up the climax.
- **Climax (21–32):** full tutti. The theme is triple-octave (flute 8va, violin I + oboe, horn 1 8vb), with horn 2/clarinet/bassoon filling the harmony and the contrabass doubling the cello an octave down. Timpani drive in quarters. A borrowed A-flat-minor chord (iv) darkens the peak before Fm–Bb7.
- **Resolution (33–41):** the dynamics fall back. The horn, then violin I, restate the theme quietly, with an Ab–Abm–Eb plagal glow and a final sustained E-flat chord over timpani.
- **Craft checks (in code):** the four-part skeleton is free of parallel fifths/octaves and has no hidden outer-voice perfects by leap. Leading tones resolve and chord sevenths resolve down. All parts stay within idiomatic ranges (flute ≤ C7, horns F2–F5, timpani Eb2–C3, contrabass ≥ E1). Revision: I removed duplicate timpani hits on the roll bars (bars 20 and 32) and moved the intro inner voices off a viola/violin-II unison doubling. The flute folds down an octave above C7.
