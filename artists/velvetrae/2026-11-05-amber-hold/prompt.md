# Composing prompt — Velvet Rae, "Amber Hold"
Original quiet-storm / late-night neo-soul, 80 BPM in 4/4 with ~17% swung 16ths (kick early, snare late), Db major lifting a half step to D major for the final chorus and outro. A clean electric-guitar hook (Ab–Bb–Db, falling C–Bb) is teased on Rhodes in the intro, inverted as Db–Bb–Ab climb Bb–Db in the verses, climbed as a rising sequence in the pre-chorus, stated and stretched in every chorus, reharmonized over an Emaj9–Ebm9–Dmaj9 chromatic descent in the bridge, transposed to D and restated in the final chorus with oohs harmony, and played softly in the ritardando outro. Rhodes with voice-led maj9/min11/13/7#9 stabs that change rhythm every bar, finger bass laid back behind the kick with chromatic approaches, sparse clean-guitar skanks in the choruses, warm pad, soft strings. Chords: Dbmaj9, Cm11–F13, Bbm11, Ebm9–Ab13, Gbmaj9, F7#9, Bbm6–Eb13 (minor iv), G13 tritone sub, Ab13sus–Ab7b9, C7alt, D13sus–D7#9 pivot into D. Cross-stick verses, ghost snares, shaker from verse 2, open hats and light claps in choruses, pre-chorus snare-roll builds, bridge half-time breakdown that rebuilds, tambourine and louder lead in the final chorus.
