# Composing prompt — Velvet Rae, "Copper Softly"
Original mid-tempo after-hours neo-soul / quiet R&B, 72 BPM in 4/4 with ~18% swung 16ths (kick early, snare late), C# minor with dorian color lifting to relative E major for the final chorus and outro. A muted-trumpet hook (E–G#–B falling A#–G#) — rising minor third then fifth, falling second then second — is teased on Rhodes in the intro, inverted as B–G#–E climb G#–B in the verses, climbed as a rising sequence in the pre-chorus, stated and stretched in every chorus, reharmonized over an Amaj9–G#7alt–C#m11–F#13 chromatic turn in the bridge, transposed into E major and restated in the final chorus with oohs harmony, and played softly in the ritardando outro. Rhodes with voice-led maj9/min11/13/7#9 stabs that change rhythm every bar, finger bass laid back behind the kick with chromatic approaches, sparse clean-guitar skanks in the choruses, warm pad, soft strings. Chords: C#m11, F#13, Bmaj9, Emaj9, Amaj9#11, G#7alt, D#m7b5–G#7b9, Amaj9, F#m11–B13, C#m9–F#13, G#13sus–G#7b9, D7#9 tritone sub, B13sus–B7#9 pivot into E. Cross-stick verses, ghost snares, shaker from verse 2, open hats and light claps in choruses, pre-chorus snare-roll builds, bridge half-time breakdown that rebuilds, tambourine and louder lead in the final chorus.
