## How it differs from Amber Window / Sunday Kettle
- Key C minor (vs D minor / F major), slowest so far at 74 BPM (vs 80 / 86).
- Chord palette: Cm9 – Abmaj9 – Fm11 – G7#9 with a borrowed Dbmaj7 and Bb13 turnarounds in B (no ii-V loops from before).
- Instruments: fingerpicked nylon guitar + warm pad + upright bass, kalimba & flute leads (no Rhodes, vibes, piano, or muted guitar).
- Mood: late-night commute / city lights, between the rainy evening of Amber Window and the sunny morning of Sunday Kettle.

- Intro (1-4): nylon guitar arpeggios alone, filter opening; hats tick in at bar 3.
