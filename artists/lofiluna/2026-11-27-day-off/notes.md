## How it differs from earlier songs
- First D major song; 87 BPM not used before.
- First whistle lead; FM bell keys instead of Rhodes/guitar; upright bass two-feel; soft kit.
- Bright major turnaround (I – vi – ii – V – iii – VI7b9 – ii – V); B starts on IV and ends on a sus V. No IV–iv move (used in Snow Day).
