# Creative notes — Satellite Lullaby (Nova Drift)

- Concept: a satellite drifting through one slow orbit, sung to sleep. Wistful and weightless, between the night drives (Neon Tidepool, Halogen Rain) and the dawn (Glasswing Sunrise).
- Key/tempo: C# minor, 84 BPM, 4/4, 56 bars (~2:42 with tail).
- Progression: C#m9 – Amaj9 – Emaj7 – Badd9; Dark Side turns to Amaj9 – F#m9 – Dmaj7#11 – G#7sus, with G#7 leading back home.
- Structure: Launch Window (1–12, pads and space-voice choir swell, music box sings the lullaby, arp creeps in at half speed) →
  Low Orbit (13–28, 16th square arp with a dotted-8th echo, octave synth bass, 808 hats then full kit, calliope lead from bar 21) →
  Dark Side (29–40, drums drop to a heartbeat kick, bass holds long roots, choir and music box swell and fall back) →
  Earthrise (41–56, fullest point, lead up an octave, crashes at 41 and 49, slow fade from bar 51 onto the home chord).
- Sounds (GeneralUser GS): Warm Pad 89, Space Voice 91, Square Lead 80 (arp + echo), Synth Bass 38, Music Box 10, Calliope 82, TR-808 kit.
- First pass: the intro came in about 20 dB under Low Orbit, Earthrise was no louder than Low Orbit, Dark Side sat flat, the 808 kick and bass crowded the sub, and the top end was dull.
- Revised: the intro is louder and the choir is under it from bar 1, the top pad voices are up an octave for air, the bass and kick are pulled back, Low Orbit builds from bar 21, the Earthrise lead and choir are pushed, and Dark Side swells and falls back.
- Master: -16.1 LUFS, LRA 10 LU, peak -1.0 dBFS, no clipping, 162.5 s, MP3 192k. Section RMS: -24.5 / -18.3 / -22 / -15 dB at the peak, then the fade.
