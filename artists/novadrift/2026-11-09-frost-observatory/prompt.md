# Frost Observatory

G major · 72 BPM · 28 bars · ambient / synthwave

Glacial ambient synthwave in G major at 72 BPM: starlight over a frozen observatory dome. Warm pad bed, bowed-glass and halo pads up high, a crystal arpeggio like ice sparkle, soft synth bass and a square lead that doubles an octave up in the aurora finale. Sections: Dome Opens, Starfield, Polar Hush, Aurora Line. Patient, luminous, wide.
