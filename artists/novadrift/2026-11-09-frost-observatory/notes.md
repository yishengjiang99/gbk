# Frost Observatory — creative notes

Concept: a frozen observatory dome opening onto a clear polar sky. Cold starlight, ice sparkle, then a slow aurora that rises and fades.

Key / tempo: G major, 72 BPM, 4/4, 28 bars (~93s + short tail). Chosen to contrast earlier Nova Drift tracks: first G major piece, slowest tempo so far besides Glasswing Sunrise, and a bright glacial mood instead of night-city neon.

Structure:
1. Dome Opens: warm pad swells in from near silence.
2. Starfield: crystal arpeggio and synth bass set a slow pulse; square lead traces the melody.
3. Polar Hush: energy drops; pads hang in the cold air.
4. Aurora Line: lead doubles an octave up, then everything fades out together.

Revisions after first listen (v1 to v2):
- v1: pads, bowed glass, halo pad and arp all sat in the same band (MIDI 59–76), so it sounded muddy and not icy; the piece ended at full level with no fade.
- Edit: moved bowed glass, halo pad and the crystal arp up an octave for shimmer (upper-mid energy up about 4 dB); octave-doubled the lead in Aurora Line; lowered bass, drums and the warm pad slightly; added an intro swell over bars 1–6 and a fade over bars 26–28.
