# Violet Harbor — creative notes

Concept: a quiet harbor swallowed by violet neon fog. Sodium lamps smear into the haze; a slow synthwave pulse drifts across the water and blooms once before dissolving.

Key / tempo: B minor, 90 BPM, 4/4, 36 bars (~96s + short tail). Chosen to contrast prior Nova Drift tracks (no reuse of their key+tempo+mood combos).

Structure:
1. Drift In — low pads and restrained drums enter the fog.
2. Fogline — saw arpeggio and bass lock the pulse; lead sketches a distant call.
3. Quay Still — energy dips; pads hang; space for the harbor to breathe.
4. Neon Bloom — lead doubles up an octave; expression crest; soft fade-out.

Revisions after first listen (v1 → v2):
- First render: pads, arp, and harmony all sat in a narrow mid band (MIDI ~57–67), so the saw arp was buried and the mix felt muddy; end decrescendo across bars 33–36 crushed the Bloom too early.
- Edit: transpose Choir, Guitar (sweep), and Piano (arp) +12 semitones for air and sparkle; octave-orchestrate Flute lead in Neon Bloom; raise bass/lead volumes, ease drums; hold expression through bars 29–33 then fade only 34–36.
