# Afterglow Interstate (Nova Drift)

Triumphant sunset outrun drive: A major, 116 BPM, 4/4, 48 bars. Polysynth pad, space-voice choir and a sweep pad stacked an octave apart, a saw-lead arpeggio, round synth bass, a bright synth lead and steady drums. Four sections: On-Ramp (pads swell in), Open Road (arp, bass and drums lock in under the lead), Overpass (a lighter breath before the last stretch), Afterglow (fullest point, the lead doubled an octave up, then a fade as the sun goes down).
