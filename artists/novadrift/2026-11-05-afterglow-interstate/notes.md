# Creative notes — Afterglow Interstate (Nova Drift)

- Concept: the last stretch of a highway drive at sunset, windows down. Warmer and more open-hearted than the earlier night drives (Neon Tidepool, Halogen Rain).
- Key/tempo: A major, 116 BPM, 4/4, 48 bars (~1:42 with tail). The fastest and brightest Nova Drift track so far.
- Structure: On-Ramp (1–8, pads swell in) → Open Road (9–24, saw arp, synth bass and drums under the lead) → Overpass (25–32, lighter breath) → Afterglow (33–48, fullest point, lead doubled an octave up, fade over the last four bars).
- Sounds (GeneralUser GS): Polysynth 90, Space Voice 91, Saw Lead 81 (arp), Synth Bass 38, Bass+Lead 87, Sweep Pad 95, standard kit.
- First pass: all three pads sat in the same narrow register, so the mids were thin and the low end dominated; the lead was buried and the ending stopped flat.
- Revised: choir and sweep pad moved up an octave, bass and drums pulled back, lead pushed and doubled an octave up in Afterglow, pads crescendo through the intro and everything fades over bars 45–48.
- Master: server render normalized to -17.6 LUFS, peak -1.0 dBTP, no clipping, MP3 192k. Section RMS: -25 / -21 / -25 / -19 dB, then the fade.
- Composed with the gbk MCP server (create, two edit passes, server render). All material is original.
