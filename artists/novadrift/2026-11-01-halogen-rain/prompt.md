# Prompt — Halogen Rain (Nova Drift)

Restless rain-soaked synthwave at 2am: D minor, 104 BPM, 4/4, 64 bars. Warm pad and sweep pad slowly swelling out of the rain, a saw-lead arpeggio pulsing under halogen streetlights, synth bass, soft retro drums fading in, a square-lead melody that varies on the last pass. Four sections: Wet Streetlights (pads and lead, drums creeping in), Halogen Drive (arp, bass and drums at full pulse), Underpass (pulled back, darker), Rain Lifts (fullest point, the melody opens up, then a long fade as the rain stops).
