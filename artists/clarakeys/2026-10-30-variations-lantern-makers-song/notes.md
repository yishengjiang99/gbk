# Creative notes: Theme and Variations in D Minor, The Lantern-Maker's Song (Clara Keys)

- **Theme (1-8):** an 8-bar period in D minor at about 58 bpm: i, V6/5, i, III, VI, iiø7, V7, i. The song-like melody runs A4 to A5 with a PAC.
- **Var I:** the theme in the RH over flowing LH eighth-note broken chords.
- **Var II:** sixteenth-note diminution (neighbour turns and steps toward each next note), slightly faster.
- **Var III (Maggiore):** D major cantabile (I, V6/5, I, iii, vi, ii7, V7, I) over wide arpeggios, quieter.
- **Var IV (agitato):** the theme moves to the LH in bass and tenor octaves under offbeat RH triplet chords, at f.
- **Var V:** the climax. Melody in RH octaves over hammered LH octaves, ff.
- **Coda:** a D pedal under a descending, fragmented version of the theme (iv6/4, V7, i, VI, iv, V7, i), with a long rit to pp. The ending stays in minor on purpose.
